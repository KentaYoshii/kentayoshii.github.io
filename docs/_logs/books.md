# Books

Source of truth for the Books page. `scripts/merge_books.py` reads the
`- _Title_ by Author` lines below and merges them with the Goodreads
export into `docs/_data/books.json`.

A `## <year>` heading sets the year read for everything under it.
`## Undated` is the pre-2024 backlog, which has no per-book date.
The `###` subheadings are for reading only -- books are recorded at
year granularity, so the month is not parsed.
